# A Letter to Mom

After two weeks, Leo began to recover. His arm was not broken, and the treatment at the hospital worked well. His leg did not bleed anymore. Minh was also well again, so he sat down and wrote a long email to his mother in Vietnam.

> Dear Mom,

> Don't worry, I am fine now. I was sick, but I didn't faint or anything. Let me tell you about my new family.

> Tom is tall and a little overweight, because he loves pizza. He is going bald, so he always wears a hat. He doesn't care about his looks. His shirts are very **plain**, and his room is untidy. But he is honest, cheerful and very generous. He is always helpful when I have a problem.

> Anna is slim and **elegant**. She cares about her appearance and always looks smart. Her desk is very neat. She is a teacher, so she is a little strict, but she is fair. She is calm and **sensible**. When there is a problem, she thinks first and then acts.

> Grandma Rose is the oldest, but in many ways she is the youngest! She has a lot of **wrinkles**, and her hands are **wrinkled** too, but her eyes are bright. She is very **wise**. When I am sad, she says something small, and I feel better. She is gentle, but she is also brave. Last year she went up a mountain!

> The twins are fifteen. They are the same height, but they are very different.

> Mia has **wavy** red hair and **freckles** on her nose. She is cute, but don't tell her I said that! She is very talented. She plays the piano and draws beautiful pictures. She is confident and not shy at all. But she is **impatient**. When the bus is late, she gets angry. Her room is messy, with clothes everywhere.

> Leo is quiet and **sensitive**. He is a little shy with new people, and he worries about his weight because he wants to be a football player. He is not stupid, but he can be careless with his homework. When he was little, he was very naughty, Grandma says.

> Some people in the town are rude to foreigners, but most are very kind. Mia says the boys at school think she is attractive, but she says those boys are silly. Tom says she is crazy about drawing and has no time for boys.

> Yesterday, Leo was a bit selfish and ate all the cake. Mia was angry. But Grandma Rose said, "Life is too short to be serious about cake." Everybody laughed, even Mia.

> I think I am lucky, Mom. Please don't work too hard. Give my love to Dad.

> Your son,
> Minh

Minh read the email again before he sent it. He thought about his own family. His mother was like Anna, he thought, and his father was a bit like Tom. Then he pressed "Send" and went downstairs. Somebody was making pancakes.
