# Moving Day

Sam moved into his new studio on a cold Saturday. Minh and Leo came to help. The room was empty except for the old armchair and the couch. "I need everything," Sam said. "A bed, a table... everything."

They went to a second-hand furniture shop. It was big and full of old things. Sam bought a **mattress** for his bed, a **bookshelf** for his books, and a **wardrobe** for his clothes. "I don't need a **closet**," he said. "The wardrobe is enough." He also found a small kitchen **cabinet** with a **drawer** for forks and knives.

"You need something on the floor," Leo said. "It's cold." So Sam bought a big **carpet** for the room and a small **rug** for the bathroom.

There was only one window, and it had no **curtains**. "I can't sleep in the light," Sam said. He bought a long blue **curtain**. The shop also had a **blind** for the kitchen window. "One curtain is enough for me," said Sam. "I'm poor."

Then Minh's phone rang. It was Anna. "If you're at the second-hand shop, can you look for a baby's bed? My sister is having a baby next month."

"Do you mean a **crib**?" asked Minh.

"Yes. Or in England we say a **cot**," Anna said.

They found a white wooden crib. It was perfect. Leo also saw a **bunk** bed. "I always wanted a bunk bed when I was a kid," he said. "Mia and I wanted to sleep on the top bunk."

Back in the studio, they carried everything up. Sam put a **sheet** on the mattress and a warm **blanket** on top. His mother sent him a **duvet** from Spain, and he put two **pillows** on the bed. On the old couch, he put a red **cushion**. "Now it feels like home," he said.

In the kitchen, there was not much. There was an old **fridge** and a very small **freezer** for ice. There was an old **cooker** too. "Is that a **stove** or an **oven**?" asked Leo.

"Both," said Sam. "You cook on the top, and you bake in the bottom."

"In America, they call a fridge a **refrigerator**," Minh said. "It's the same thing, but a longer word."

There was no **microwave** and no **dishwasher**. "So I wash everything in the **sink**," Sam said. "It's good exercise."

At the end of the day, they were very tired. There were only two chairs, so Leo sat on a **stool** and Minh sat on an old wooden **bench** by the window. They ate pizza from the box. Outside, it started to snow.

"Thank you, guys," Sam said. "This is the best room in the world."

Minh looked around. The room was small and the furniture was old, but it was warm and full of laughter. He agreed.
