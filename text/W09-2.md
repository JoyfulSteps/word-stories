# The Box in the Attic

One rainy Sunday, Grandma Rose said, "Minh, can you help me? I need a box from the **attic**."

The house was old and big. Downstairs there was a long **hall** with photos on the walls. Upstairs, a narrow **hallway** went past all the bedrooms. At the end of it, there was a small door and some steps up to the attic. Under the house, there was a dark **basement**, and Tom kept wine in an old **cellar** there. "This house is like a hotel," Minh said. "It even has a **corridor**."

The attic was dusty and full of boxes. Minh found the right one and carried it down to the **lounge**. Mia came to look.

Grandma Rose opened the box slowly. Inside, there were old clothes and many small things. First, she took out a blue **jumper**. "I wore this when I was twenty," she said. "It's pure **wool**. My mother made it." It had big white **buttons** and a small **pocket** on the front.

Then she took out a dress. It was made of **silk**, soft and shiny. It had a high **collar** and long **sleeves**. "This was my best dress," Grandma said. "In 1965, it was the height of **fashion**." Mia held it up. "Grandma, this is beautiful. You had great **style**!"

There was also a **leather** jacket with a **zip**. "In America we say **zipper**," said Tom from the door. "We know, Dad," said Mia.

At the bottom of the box, they found a small **handbag** and an old **purse**. Inside the purse was a little box. And inside that box was **jewelry**: a gold **necklace**, two pearl **earrings**, a silver **bracelet**, and a **ring**.

Grandma Rose went quiet. She took the ring and held it in her hand. "My husband gave me this ring," she said softly. "I lost it thirty years ago. I looked for it everywhere. And it was in my purse, in the attic, all this time."

She put the ring on her finger. There was a tear in her eye, but she was smiling.

"Tell us the story," Mia said.

"Well," said Grandma, "it was summer. I was on the beach in a **cotton** dress and **sunglasses**. Your grandfather came to me, very nervous, and he dropped his **wallet** in the sand. All his money went everywhere. I helped him pick it up, and we laughed. A year later, we were married."

Mia was quiet. Then she said, "That's the best story I've ever heard."

Later, Minh went upstairs. He started to **undress** for bed. He put his jeans on the chair and his **backpack** on the floor. He thought about Grandma's ring, and about his own grandmother in Vietnam. Then he picked up his phone and called her.
