# The Night the Lights Went Out

In December, a big storm came. The wind was very strong, and the rain hit the windows all evening. At eight o'clock, there was a loud noise, and suddenly all the lights went out. The TV went off. The **heater** stopped. The whole house was dark.

"Don't worry," said Tom. "Where are the **candles**?"

Anna found some candles in a kitchen drawer. She put them on the table in old **jars** and a small glass **vase**. The room looked beautiful in the yellow light.

Tom tried the light **switch**. Nothing. He checked the **bulb** in the lamp. It was fine. He took out the **plug** and put it in another **socket**. Nothing. "It's not our house," he said. "It's the whole street."

The house got cold quickly. In Vietnam, Minh had a **fan** in his room to stay cool, not a heater. Now he wanted a heater more than anything. "The **radiators** are getting cold," Mia said.

"Then we use the old way," said Grandma Rose. She went to the **fireplace** in the living room. Nobody used it anymore. Above it, there was an old **poster** of London and a family photo in a gold **frame**. Grandma Rose put some wood in the fireplace and lit a fire. Soon the room was warm.

"Now, tea," said Grandma Rose. But there was no electricity for the kettle. So she filled a big **pot** with water from the **tap**.

"We call it a **faucet** in America," said Tom.

"Not in my house," Grandma said. She put a **lid** on the pot and hung it over the fire, like in the old days.

While the water got hot, Anna found some food. There was no light in the kitchen, so she brought a **tray** with everything on it: bread, cheese, a **tin** of fish, and a **jug** of milk. Leo found an old **pan** and tried to cook eggs on the fire. The first egg fell into the fire. Everybody laughed.

When the tea was ready, Grandma Rose made it the English way. She gave Minh a cup on a small **saucer**. "A real cup, not a **mug**," she said. "This is a special night."

They sat around the fire and told stories. Tom told them about the big storm in America when he was a boy. Grandma told them about the war. Minh told them about the rainy season in Vietnam. "Sometimes the water comes into the house," he said, "and we put all the **furniture** up high."

At midnight, the lights came back on. The TV started talking, and the heater made a noise. "Oh no," said Mia. "I liked it better in the dark."

Then the fire **alarm** in the hall started to beep, because of the smoke from the fireplace. Tom ran to stop it.

The next morning, there were dirty **dishes** everywhere. Minh washed every **dish** in the kitchen, and then he washed his face in the small **washbasin** in the bathroom. He was tired, but happy. It was the best night since he came to England.
