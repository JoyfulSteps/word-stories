# The First Day

On Monday, Minh started college. The night before, he could not sleep. He was nervous and a little worried. "What if nobody talks to me?" he asked Mia.

"Don't worry," she said. "You're friendly. People will like you."

In the morning, he took off his **pajamas** and put on jeans and a **hoodie**. He looked in the mirror. He felt uncomfortable. "Is this OK?" he asked.

Tom looked at him. "In America, students wear sneakers and a hoodie every day. You look fine."

"In England, we say trainers, not sneakers," said Grandma Rose. She was sitting in her **slippers** with a cup of tea. "And you need a scarf and gloves. It's cold."

So Minh wore a scarf, a **cardigan** from Grandma, a coat, and one glove. He could not find the other glove.

At college, there was a big surprise. In his first class, the teacher asked everybody to stand up and talk about their country. Minh's face went red. He was **embarrassed**, and his voice was shaking. But when he finished, the class clapped. One student, a tall boy from Spain, said, "That was amazing. I want to visit Vietnam!" His name was Sam. Minh was surprised and pleased. Suddenly he did not feel lonely.

After class, Sam and Minh had lunch together. Sam was very bright. He was intelligent and creative. He studied design. But he was also modest. He never talked about his good grades.

In the afternoon, it started to rain. Minh had no **raincoat**. He was wet and cold, and his mood went down. "What an awful day," he thought. "What horrible weather." But Sam was laughing in the rain. "In Spain, rain is lucky!" he said. Their laughter made Minh feel better.

On Saturday, Mia took Minh shopping for winter clothes. They went to a big shop. Minh tried on a **vest** and some **underwear**, because he did not bring enough from Vietnam. Mia tried on a blouse and a belt. In the shop window, there was a man's suit with a red tie. "You need that for parties," Mia said.

"No, I'm a student," Minh said. "I am not interested in suits."

Near the door, they saw summer clothes: **sandals** and a **swimsuit**, on sale. "Who buys a swimsuit in November?" Minh asked. Mia laughed.

Then Mia saw her friend Chloe. Chloe was with Leo's girlfriend. They talked and laughed, and Minh waited. Mia came back looking unhappy.

"What's wrong? You look upset," Minh said.

"Chloe has a new phone. I wish I had one," Mia said. "I'm a bit **jealous**. My phone is old."

"Are you mad at her?"

"No, not cross. Just disappointed in myself, I think. It's a silly feeling."

On the way home, a big dog ran out and barked at them. Mia was **frightened**. She held Minh's arm. For a moment she showed real fear. Then she laughed. "That was unlucky," she said. "First Chloe, then a dog!"

At home, Leo was wearing his football **uniform**. He was proud. He was back in the team. "Look," he said. "I'm not worried about my leg anymore." Everyone felt relaxed and comfortable. It was a good end to a long week.
