# Kate's Wedding

Kate and Ben's wedding was in June, by the river. Everybody was invited, and everybody had the same problem: what to wear.

"I have nothing," said Mia, standing in front of her wardrobe full of clothes. "Absolutely nothing."

"You have a hundred things," said Leo.

"Nothing **trendy**," said Mia. "Nothing that goes with my new style."

So Mia and Anna went shopping. Minh went too, because he also needed an **outfit**. He had never been to an English wedding. "What do people wear?" he asked.

"Men wear a suit," said Anna. "Maybe a **waistcoat**. Women wear a dress and **heels**. And a hat, if they're brave."

At the shop, Mia tried on everything. A **sleeveless** dress with **lace** at the top. A **spotted** dress with black dots. A **checked** dress that made her look like a picnic blanket. A **tight** dress that she couldn't breathe in. A **loose**, **baggy** dress that made her look like she was wearing a tent.

"This is impossible," said Mia. She was getting **tense**. "Everything is wrong."

"Try this," said Anna. She held up a simple green dress made of **linen**. It was light and beautiful. Mia put it on. She looked in the mirror. She smiled.

"OK," she said. "This one."

Anna bought **tights** to go with it. "Not **stockings**," said Mia. "Stockings are for old ladies." Anna said nothing. She was wearing stockings.

Minh was easier. He didn't want to spend too much. He found a dark blue suit in a second-hand shop. It was a little big, but Grandma Rose said she could fix it. "I'm not **keen** on shopping," he said. "I just want something that fits."

"You're **desperate** to leave, aren't you?" said Mia.

"Very desperate."

Grandma Rose sat at her old sewing machine for two evenings. She made the suit perfect. "In my day, we made everything ourselves," she said. "Every **fabric**, every **material**. **Denim** for work, **nylon** for stockings, velvet for parties. Now nobody knows how to sew."

On the day of the wedding, the weather was perfect. Everyone arrived at the river in their best clothes. Tom wore a gray suit and an old **overcoat**, even though it was warm. "It's my lucky coat," he said. Leo wore a **casual** jacket and jeans because he still couldn't wear smart shoes with his knee. Hana wore a pale blue dress. Minh thought she looked beautiful. He did not say it. Mia said it for him: "Minh thinks you look beautiful." Minh wanted to disappear.

The wedding was simple and lovely. Kate wore a white dress with a long lace veil. Ben wore a dark suit and was so nervous that his hands were shaking. Their friends read poems. Ben's sister, now with a baby in her arms, cried through the whole thing.

After the ceremony, there was a party in a big tent by the river. The food was cooked by Daniel and Pat. Pat wore a white **apron** over her best dress and refused to sit down. "Somebody has to make sure the food is right," she said.

In the evening, it started to rain a little. Hana didn't have a coat, so Minh gave her his jacket. She pulled the **hood** of her own little cardigan over her head too, but it didn't help much. They danced in the rain while everyone else watched from the tent.

"You're both crazy," said Mia.

"I **appreciate** the comment," said Minh. "Now go away."

Later, Grandma Rose found Minh at the edge of the tent. "Don't let me **bother** you," she said. "I just want to say one thing. I have a **concern**."

Minh was **concerned**. "What is it?"

"My concern," she said, "is that you might wait too long. Life is short. If you love someone, tell them. Don't be like me and Margaret."

Minh looked at Hana, dancing with Mia in the rain. He nodded. "I won't wait," he said.

"Good," said Grandma Rose. "Now go and dance with her again. I'm too old to watch you stand in a corner."
