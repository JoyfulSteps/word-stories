# The School Trip

When Minh was eleven, his class went on a school trip to the city zoo. It was the most exciting day of the year. The last school trip was two years ago, to a park with a small hill, and everybody wanted to go again. The night before, Minh checked the weather on TV. "Tomorrow will be sunny and warm," the weather man said. "No rain, no wind." Minh was very happy.

But in the morning, the sky was gray and cloudy. By eight o'clock, it was windy and cool. By nine, there was rain. It was a rainy day after all. "At least it isn't snow," the teacher said. In their town, it never snowed. Minh only saw snowy places on TV. The children sat on the bus, wet and sad. "The weather man was wrong," Tuan said.

Then, after an hour, the rain stopped. The clouds went away and the sun came out. Everything was wet and green: the trees, the grass, the flowers. The air smelled clean. Soon the roads were dry again. Even the field next to the road looked beautiful.

At the zoo, they saw many animals. First, they saw the big ones: an elephant, a lion and a tiger. The tiger walked up and down behind the glass. "It looks angry," Lan's friend said. Then they saw a bear, sleeping on a rock, and a monkey that took a girl's hat.

Next, there were animals from other parts of the world. There was a tall **giraffe** with a very long neck. It ate the leaves at the top of a tree. There was a **zebra** with black and white lines. Tuan said, "Is it a white horse with black lines, or a black horse with white lines?" Nobody knew.

There was a **kangaroo** from Australia. It jumped very high. And there was a black and white **panda** from China, eating plants and sitting like a fat old man. Everybody loved the panda. It did nothing at all, but all the children watched it for twenty minutes.

Minh's favorite place was the small house for little animals. There was a snake on a stone and a frog in the sand, a spider in a glass box, and a beautiful room full of flowers and **butterflies**. A yellow **butterfly** sat on Minh's hand. He stood very still. "Don't move," the teacher said quietly. After a few seconds, it flew away.

"What is it?" Minh asked. "Is it a kind of **insect**?"

"Yes," said the teacher. "A butterfly is an insect, like a bee. Insects have six legs."

In the afternoon, they visited a small farm next to the zoo. There was a cow, a pig, some sheep, a duck and a family of rabbits. A small mouse ran across the floor, and three girls screamed. "Mice are more afraid of you than you are of them," said the farmer.

At the end of the day, the teacher showed them a video about the sea. There were sandy beaches, islands and a smart gray **dolphin** that could jump through a ring. "This is the world," she said. "The earth is very big. There are mountains, forests, rivers, lakes and seas. And everywhere there are animals."

On the bus home, the moon was already in the sky. Minh looked out of the window at the first star. He thought about the giraffe and the panda and the dolphin. They lived so far away. "One day," he thought, "I want to see the world."

At home, the family dog and cat were waiting for him. He told them about his day. The cat was not interested. The dog was. Outside, a small bird sang in the tree. "Dogs are the best pets," Minh decided.
