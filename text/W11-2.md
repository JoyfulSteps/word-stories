# The Cooking Lesson

After Mia's birthday, the Parker family had a new idea. "Minh," Anna said, "can you teach us to cook a real Vietnamese meal?"

"Of course!" said Minh. He was happy. Finally, he could do something for them.

On Sunday afternoon, everyone came to the kitchen. Minh wrote the **recipe** on a piece of paper: pho, Vietnamese beef noodle soup. "First, we make the soup," he said. "This takes a long time."

He showed them how to **roast** the onions and ginger over the fire on the stove until they were black. Mia was surprised. "You **burn** them on purpose?" "Yes," said Minh. "It gives the soup a deep **flavor**."

Then he put the beef bones in a big pot of water. "Now we **boil** it," he said. "Then we wait." He **added** spices: star anise, a stick of cinnamon and some salt. Soon the whole house smelled wonderful. Grandma Rose came in from the garden. "What is that? It smells like a holiday," she said.

While the soup cooked, Minh gave everyone a job. Leo had to **chop** the green onions. Mia had to **peel** the ginger. Tom had to cut the beef into thin **slices**. Anna had to **mix** the sauce. Grandma had to **stir** the soup slowly with a big spoon and taste it.

Tom wanted to help more. "In America, we **grill** everything," he said. "Can we put the beef on the barbecue?" "No," said Minh. "In pho, the hot soup cooks the beef."

Leo wanted to **fry** something. Minh let him fry some small spring rolls. Mia wanted to **bake** a cake, but Minh said, "Not today. Today is soup day."

After four hours, the soup was ready. Minh put rice noodles in each bowl, then the raw beef, then he **poured** the hot soup over it. The beef changed color in the **heat**. On the table, there were plates of **fresh** herbs, lime and chili.

"Try it," said Minh.

Everybody took a **sip** of the soup. Nobody said anything. Then Tom said, "Wow."

"Is it good?" Minh asked.

"It's not good," said Tom. "It's amazing. It's the **tastiest** soup I have ever had."

Grandma Rose tried it carefully. "It's a little **salty**," she said. Then she added lime. "Now it's perfect."

Leo put a lot of chili in his bowl. His face went red. "It's very **spicy**!" he shouted, and drank a whole glass of water. Mia laughed so much that she almost fell off her chair.

Anna tried a piece of the ginger. "Oh, that's **bitter**," she said. "Don't eat it alone," Minh laughed. "It's for the **taste** of the soup."

After dinner, Tom opened a **beer**, and Anna had a glass of red **wine**. Grandma drank **mineral** water. Mia made a strawberry **smoothie**, and Leo made a chocolate **milkshake** and drank it with a **straw**, very loudly. Minh made hot **cocoa** for everyone who wanted it.

The ice cream in the freezer started to **melt** because Leo left the door open. Nobody cared.

"Minh," Anna said, "you have to write all your recipes in a book. You can be a chef."

"Maybe," said Minh. "But my grandmother is the real chef. I only know one soup."

"Then teach us another one next week," said Tom. And he took a second **loaf** of bread to eat with the rest of the soup, which was not Vietnamese at all, but nobody said anything.
