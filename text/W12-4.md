# The Scientist on the Farm

On the last day in Wales, Gareth and Megan's daughter came home from the city. Her name was Ffion. She was a **scientist**. She worked in a **laboratory** at a university, where she did research on **solar** **power**.

"So you make energy from the sun?" asked Minh.

"Yes," said Ffion. "Our lab tries to make better solar panels. We do experiments every day. Most of them fail. But sometimes we discover something new. One small discovery can change the world."

Leo was more interested in space. "Did you ever want to be an **astronaut**?" he asked.

"When I was ten, yes! I wanted to fly in a **rocket** to Mars," Ffion said. "Then I learned I get sick in cars. So I chose chemistry, physics and biology instead."

After lunch, Ffion showed them the new solar panels on the farm roof. "These give us electricity for the whole house," she said. "We have an electric car, too. No more gas for the car. And in winter, we use less."

"Why is it important?" asked Mia.

Ffion looked at the green hills. "Because of **pollution**," she said. "The air and the water are getting dirty. The global temperature is rising. Every year, people destroy forests and damage the planet. We need to protect nature and save energy. The result of doing nothing is terrible."

"But what can we do?" asked Mia. "We are just kids."

"A lot," Ffion said. "**Recycle** your bottles and paper. Don't **waste** food or water. And pick up **litter**."

So that afternoon, they walked along the road with big bags and picked up litter: plastic bottles, cans, paper. In two hours, they had four big bags. "People are disgusting," said Leo. But he worked very hard.

Then Megan asked for help in the garden. There were **weeds** everywhere, between the flowers and the vegetables. "Pull them out, roots and all," she said. "Weeds grow faster than anything."

Minh worked next to Ffion. She told him about the plants. "Look at this **cactus**," she said, pointing to a small plant in a pot in the window. "It's not from here. It's from the desert. It needs very little water. And this **bamboo** grows almost a meter a day in the right climate."

"We have a lot of bamboo in Vietnam," Minh said. "And **palm** trees by the sea."

"Here we have **oak** and **pine**," Ffion said, pointing to the big trees on the hill. "The oak is the king of British trees."

Mia **picked** some flowers for Grandma Rose. She was careful not to break the **stem**. "In a week, these will **bloom**," Megan said, showing her a white bud. "When it opens, each **petal** is like silk."

In the evening, Ffion and Minh talked about inventions. "What is the best invention in history?" Minh asked.

"The wheel? The machine? The computer?" Ffion said. "Many people say the robot is the future. But I think the best one is the light bulb. Before it, people lived half their lives in the dark. Its inventor changed everything. Thomas Edison didn't invent it alone, though. Many people helped."

"I want to make something useful one day too," Minh said.

"Then study hard," said Ffion. "And keep asking questions. That's what science is. Asking questions. Like in a lab: you have a liquid, a solid, a question, and a lot of patience."

The next morning, they said goodbye. Gareth gave them eggs, Megan gave them cake, and Ffion gave Minh a book about **solar** energy. Little Minh the calf watched them leave.

"Come back in the summer," Gareth said.

"We will," said Mia. "I want to see baby Minh again."

"The cow," said Minh. "She means the cow."
