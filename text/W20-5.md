# The Personality Test

One rainy Sunday, Mia found a personality test in a magazine. "Everybody has to do it," she said. "It tells you what kind of person you are."

"That's stupid," said Leo. "Tests can't tell you anything."

"You're so **grumpy**," said Mia. "That's your result already. Grumpy."

She read the questions out loud, and everyone answered: Tom, Anna, Leo, Grandma Rose, Minh and Hana, who was visiting.

The first question was: "At a party, do you talk to everyone, or do you stay with people you know?"

"I talk to everyone," said Tom, showing the small **tattoo** of an anchor on his arm, from when he was nineteen. "I even talked to the man who did this. I'm very **outgoing** and **sociable**."

"Too sociable," said Anna. "Last Christmas, you talked to the waiter for an hour."

"He was **charming**," said Tom.

The next question: "When you play a game, do you need to win?"

"Yes," said Leo and Mia at the same time. They were both very **competitive**. Last year, they had a fight about a game of cards that lasted three days.

"I'm not competitive at all," said Grandma Rose. "I just like to win."

Everyone laughed.

"When you have a problem, do you think the future will be good or bad?"

"Good," said Minh. "I'm **optimistic**."

"Bad," said Leo. "I'm **pessimistic**. Since my knee, anyway."

"You're getting better," said Anna. "Be optimistic."

Then Mia read the results. She described everyone like a teacher. "Dad: **energetic**, **enthusiastic**, a bit **bossy**. You always want to tell everyone what to do."

"I'm not bossy," said Tom. "I'm a leader."

"Mum: **reliable**, **considerate**, **mature**. You think about other people first."

"That's true," said Tom. "She's never **unreliable**. Not once in twenty years."

"Leo," Mia went on, "**moody**, **foolish** sometimes, a little **immature**."

"Hey!" said Leo.

"But also **loyal** and **determined**. When you want something, you never stop. Like your exercises."

Leo was quiet. That was actually nice.

"Grandma: **curious**, **cautious**, very wise. You always want to know everything, but you think before you act."

"I was not cautious when I was young," said Grandma Rose. "I was quite **irresponsible**. Ask Margaret."

"Minh," said Mia, "**ambitious**, **flexible**, hard-working. You want to do a lot in life, but you can change your plans when you need to."

"And Hana," said Mia. She looked at Hana and smiled. "Kind. Calm. Patient. And very good for Minh."

"Mia!" said Minh.

Hana laughed. "Thank you. I think."

"What about you, Mia?" asked Anna.

Mia looked at her own results. "Creative. **Arrogant**," she read. "What? I'm not arrogant!"

"A little arrogant," said Leo. "You won one art prize and now you think you're Picasso."

"At least I'm not **slender** like a stick and **grumpy** like an old man," said Mia.

"At least I'm not..." Leo started.

"Children," said Anna. "Enough."

Later, when Mia and Leo were arguing about something else, Minh and Hana went for a walk by the river. "Your family is funny," said Hana.

"They're not my family," said Minh. "Well, they are. Kind of."

"Do you think the test is true?" Hana asked. "Am I good for you?"

Minh stopped walking. He looked at her. Her hair was in the wind and her cheeks were pink from the cold. He took a deep breath.

"Yes," he said. "I think you are. Very good."

Hana smiled. "I think you're good for me too."

They stood by the river for a long time. Then Hana took his hand. It was the most natural thing in the world.

Back at the house, Grandma Rose was watching from the window. "Finally," she said. "I thought he'd never do it."
